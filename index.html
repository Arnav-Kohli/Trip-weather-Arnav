Trip weather, decided

Read the sky before you pack.

A 40% chance of rain has never once told you whether to bring the coat. Give me a city and your dates: you get a plain verdict for every day, one packing list for the trip, and the single day worth spending outdoors.

    Forecast and geocoding from Open-Meteo, fetched live in your browser — no key, nothing stored, nothing cached. Advice is written for city sightseeing on foot; a trekker or a wedding guest would need different thresholds. Forecasts are reliable for about a fortnight, so the range is capped at 14 days.
